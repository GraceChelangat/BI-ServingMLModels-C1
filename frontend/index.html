BI Models: Group C1 Lab Demo

Four models from our group labs, served through one Flask API behind Gunicorn and Nginx.

Customer segment

Enter a customer's shopping behaviour to find out which segment they belong to.

Show recommender

Select the shows a viewer has watched, then get recommendations.

Financial distress check

Enter a company's profile to predict its distress category.

Daily sales forecast

Describe a retail store to predict its daily sales revenue in KES.
